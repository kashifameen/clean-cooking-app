Instant Pot Pro & Hotpot 3-7 Day Batch Prep Tips

Batch Protein Braise

Pressure cook 1kg to 1.5kg of chicken breasts or beef strips on High Pressure for 10 mins with 150ml broth. Quick shred in the inner pot and portion evenly across your meal containers.

Fluffy Batch Rice & Quinoa

Rinse 500g basmati rice, add 550ml broth (1:1.1 ratio). Pressure cook on Low for 4 mins, 10m Natural Release. Fluff with a silicone fork for perfectly tender grains that never clump when reheated.

Storage & Freshness

For a 5-day or 7-day prep, keep containers for days 1–3 in the fridge (below 4°C). Freeze containers for days 4–7 immediately after cooling to preserve peak taste, moisture, and nutritional integrity.
